Dramatis Personae

Grettir Asmundson
Called the Strong (Grettir sterki); the hero of the saga, famed for his extraordinary strength, courage, and tragic fate as an outlaw.
Asmund the Greyhaired
Grettir's father (Asmundr hörulángi).
Asdis
Bard Jokulson's daughter, Grettir's mother; a woman of strong character and prophetic foresight.
Atli Asmundson
Grettir's brother, slain by Thorbiörn Angle.
Illugi Asmundson
Grettir's younger brother, who shares his exile on Drangey and dies with him.
Onund Treefoot
Grettir's grandfather (Öndóðr tréfótr), who lost a leg in battle but remained a great warrior.
Ufeigh Grettir
Grettir's great-grandfather (Úfeigr), a powerful chieftain and lawman.
Thorbiörn Angle
Grettir's principal enemy; a cunning and vengeful man who engineers Grettir's outlawry and eventual slaying on Drangey.
Thorbiörn Oxmain
A wealthy and quarrelsome neighbour, an early antagonist of Grettir's family.
Glam
A fearsome Swedish shepherd, slain by Grettir but returning as a ghost (glámr) whose curse blights Grettir's fortunes.
Thorfinn of Haramsey
A good and generous man who shelters Grettir after Glam's haunting, and whose sons Grettir saves from drowning.
Hallmund
A mountain spirit (bergrisi) who befriends Grettir in the wilderness, father of the poet-ghost Thord Yeller.
King Olaf the Saint
King of Norway (Óláfr Haraldsson helgi), who attempts to redeem Grettir but cannot overcome his ill fortune.
Svein, Earl of Norway
Jarl of Lade, who takes Grettir into his service during the wars in Norway.
Magnus the Good
King of Norway, who grants Grettir's mother a hearing after his death.
Gudmund the Rich
A powerful chieftain of Maddervales (Gudmundr inn ríki).
Bardi Gudmundson
Called Slaying-Bardi (Bardi Guðmundsson); leads an expedition against the outlaws of Arnarvatn Heath with Grettir among them.
Skapti Thorodson
The Lawman of Iceland (Skapti Þóroddsson), one of the wisest men of his age.
Thorgils Arison
Of Reek-knolls, a notable chieftain and one of Grettir's friends in the north country.
Thorbiorg the Big
Olaf's daughter (Þorbjörg digra), a wise woman who foresees Grettir's future.
Sporlaug
A woman who loves Grettir; their courtship ends in sorrow.
Thord Yeller
A poet and ghost, son of Hallmund the mountain spirit.
Thorkell Krafla
A chieftain who offers Grettir shelter and friendship during his outlawry.
Thurid
Thorbiörn Angle's stepmother, whose treachery plays a role in Grettir's downfall.
Kormak of Meal
A violent neighbour of Thorfinn of Haramsey, and one of Grettir's adversaries.
Thorgils Makson
Slain by Grettir in a dispute at the Thing.
Haflidi
A chieftain who aids Grettir in Norway.
Biorn Hitdale-champion
A notable warrior and companion in several episodes.
Asta Gudbrand's-daughter
Mother of King Olaf the Saint.
Eric Snare
A Norwegian chieftain (Eiríkr snara).
